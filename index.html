The Kitchen

A small kitchen,
cooking to order.

Treasure House sits on Marlowes, in the centre of Hemel Hempstead. The kitchen runs two services a day, Tuesday through Sunday — lunch from noon, then a longer dinner shift into the evening. Closed Mondays, open on bank holidays.

The menu is the British-Chinese takeaway canon: hot-and-sour soup, chow mein, salt-and-pepper specials, sweet-and-sour, crispy duck. Every order is cooked from scratch, so phoning ahead is the kindest thing you can do for the cook on a Friday night.

Order by phone for collection, or browse the full menu online at thetreasurehouse.co.uk. Group orders and party platters are welcome — call ahead so the kitchen can pace it properly.

If you've not ordered here before, the popular picks are the prawn toast, the crispy shredded duck, and a portion of Singapore-style noodles to share between two.

Signatures

A handful of the
regulars' regulars.

A working menu — call to order
  • Prawn Toast
    Sesame-crusted toast topped with prawn paste and deep-fried — the classic starter.
  • Hot & Sour Soup
    The Sichuan-Cantonese standard: black vinegar, white pepper, tofu, bamboo shoots, ribboned egg.
  • Crispy Shredded Duck
    Slow-cooked duck, twice-fried until crisp, tossed with chilli and spring onion.
  • Beef Chow Mein
    Wok-tossed egg noodles with strip beef, onion and bean sprouts. The house benchmark.
  • Singapore-Style Noodles
    Thin rice noodles, curry-warmed, with prawn, char siu and a tumble of vegetables. Made to share.
  • Szechuan Chicken
    Diced chicken in a chilli-forward, garlic-bright sauce. Ask if you'd like it dialled hotter.
  • Orange Chicken
    Crisp-battered chicken with a sticky, citrus-bright glaze.
